Our Heritage & Story

About Morning Mug

From passion for authentic roasted beans to a warm community haven in Badda, Dhaka.

Founder & Visionary

Md Sahadat Hossain

Hi, I'm Md Sahadat Hossain, founder of Morning Mug Coffee House. My journey began with a simple belief: a cup of coffee is never just a beverage—it is a cherished moment of peace, inspiration, and connection.

At Morning Mug, we are dedicated to curating artisanal roasts, freshly baked pastries, and an atmosphere where everyone feels like family. Whether you are meeting close friends, conducting creative work, or starting your morning quietly, our doors are always open for you.

Thank you for being part of our story and making Morning Mug Coffee House a vibrant neighborhood home.

Our Philosophy

The Craft Behind Every Brew

Founded on February 2, 2025, Morning Mug was born out of dedication to quality craft coffee in Dhaka. We partner with ethical coffee producers to bring single-origin beans and signature espresso blends to life.

Our culinary team crafts wholesome, handcrafted pastries and savory snacks daily without shortcuts. Each morning, our kitchen fills with the scent of fresh butter croissants, rich cinnamon rolls, and artisan espresso.
